# Doença de Crohn — Support

Free personal journal and education app for adults (18+), available in Portuguese, English and Spanish.

Support: **contato@asppect.com.br**. Please describe the issue, device and app version. Do not send medical records, prescriptions, documents or passwords by email.

Your records stay on your device. We cannot access or restore them remotely. Create a password-protected backup in More → Backup and privacy before replacing your phone. Keep the password safe; we cannot recover it.

Language: More → Settings → App language.

Water integration is optional in More → Device health. Only the water you record is written to Apple Health or Health Connect. The app does not read health data. Disconnecting stops future sends; removing sent records is a separate action. Local records remain in the app.

For a medical concern, contact your care team. In an emergency, contact local emergency services. This support email does not provide medical or emergency care.
